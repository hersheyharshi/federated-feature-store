# Writing it in your own words

The code and documents here are a starting point. Before you present this project, rewrite the parts below yourself. It makes the project yours, and it is the best way to find what you do not yet understand.

Answer each in 2 to 4 sentences, without looking.

1. What problem does this solve, and why can hospitals not just pool data?
2. What does the shared feature store share, and what does it keep private?
3. What does epsilon mean, and what did a smaller epsilon cost?
4. Why did the Switzerland result need a local offset, and why is the result still weak?
5. What did not work, and what did you learn from it?
6. What would you do next with more time?

Then put your answers in the README under a heading such as "What we learned", and in the Contributions file. If anyone asks how you built this, answer truthfully, including which tools helped.
